See who plays with whom most often. Pick your BG Stats export file and the graph is built in your browser. The file stays on your device.

Drop your BGStatsExport.json here, or choose it from your device.

–

Hours are estimates: each play uses its logged time when there is one, otherwise the average logged time for that game, otherwise the midpoint of the BoardGameGeek play-time range. Years limits the graph to that range. The full span is the whole file, including plays with no date. Names start as initials. Download image saves a picture. Copy link builds an address from the names, years, and links on screen, and that address stays on this device. Shorten link sends that address to is.gd, which stores it and hands back a short link. A very large view keeps the strongest links.
