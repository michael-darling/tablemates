See who plays with whom most often. Pick your BG Stats export file and the graph is built in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop your BGStatsExport.json here, or choose it from your device.

Hours are estimates: each play uses its logged time when there is one, otherwise the average logged time for that game, otherwise the midpoint of the BoardGameGeek play-time range. Names are anonymized to initials by default, so check the setting before you share an image.
