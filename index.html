tablemates

See who plays with whom most often. Pick your BG Stats export file and the graph is built in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop your BGStatsExport.json here, or choose it from your device.

Hours are estimates: each play uses its logged time when there is one, otherwise the average logged time for that game, otherwise the midpoint of the BoardGameGeek play-time range. Names start as initials. Download image saves a picture. Copy link builds an address that contains only the names and links on screen, and nothing is uploaded.
